A locally focused restoration company helping homes and businesses in Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge recover from water, fire, storm and mould damage.

WHO WE ARE

Who We Are

Property damage is stressful, and it's not always clear what to do next. KWC Emergency Restorations manages the full process, from initial assessment to final repair, so you have one team handling the job from start to finish.

We work directly with homeowners, businesses and property managers, and coordinate with insurance adjusters when a claim is involved.

OUR APPROACH

Accuracy, Quality & Professionalism

Every job starts with an honest assessment of the damage. We do the work properly, not just quickly, and aim to leave every property in better condition than we found it — with a clear explanation of what was done and why.
