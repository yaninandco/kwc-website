COMMON CAUSES

What Causes Water Damage?

Water damage can strike suddenly or build up slowly over time. Common causes include:

  • Burst or leaking pipes
  • Appliance and plumbing failures
  • Sewer and drain backups
  • Overflowing sinks, toilets and bathtubs
  • Storm and rainwater intrusion
  • Flooding and unexpected water emergencies

PROPERTY DAMAGE

How Water Damage Affects Your Property

Moisture can hide behind walls, under flooring and inside insulation long after the initial water event. Left untreated, it can damage drywall, flooring and cabinetry, and lead to mould growth.

RESTORATION PROCESS

Our Water Damage Restoration Process

  • Cleaning affected areas and belongings
  • Removing unsalvageable materials
  • Monitoring the drying process
  • Repairs and reconstruction
